Add `HuckleberryAPI.watch_next_nap()`, a read-only port of the app's local SweetSpot calculation exposed as an async iterator. It yields the current next-nap state as an absolute Unix timestamp in seconds (rounded to the app's 10-minute display precision) plus the wake window, its 1-based slot, and the nap schedule, then re-emits whenever `childs/{child_id}`, `sleep/{child_id}/intervals`, or a time boundary changes it, so consumers do not poll. It never writes `childs/{child_id}`. Wake-window defaults are read from Firebase Storage `settings/settings_plus.json` and cached for six hours.

Add `HuckleberryAPI.get_next_nap()` and `HuckleberryAPI.watch_next_nap()`, a read-only port of the app's local SweetSpot calculation. `get_next_nap()` returns the next nap as an absolute Unix timestamp in seconds (rounded to the app's 10-minute display precision) plus the wake window, its 1-based slot, and the nap schedule. `watch_next_nap()` yields that state and then re-emits whenever `childs/{child_id}`, `sleep/{child_id}/intervals`, or a time boundary changes it, so consumers do not poll. Neither writes `childs/{child_id}`. Wake-window defaults are read from Firebase Storage `settings/settings_plus.json` and cached for six hours.

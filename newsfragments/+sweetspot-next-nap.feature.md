Add `HuckleberryAPI.get_next_nap()`, a read-only port of the app's local SweetSpot calculation. It returns the next nap as an absolute Unix timestamp in seconds (rounded to the app's 10-minute display precision) plus the wake window, its 1-based slot, and the nap schedule, without writing `childs/{child_id}`. Wake-window defaults are read from Firebase Storage `settings/settings_plus.json` and cached for six hours.
